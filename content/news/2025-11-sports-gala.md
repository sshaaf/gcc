It has been a season to remember at Solvangsparken. From the U13s running out at their first national championships to the women's team bringing home silver from the DM, every corner of the club has punched above its weight in 2025.

At the Glostrup Sports Gala in November, the municipality recognised Glostrup Cricket Club for the breadth of that work — not a single trophy, but a club-wide haul: medals at every youth age group, a national-final run by the women, and four players called into Danish national-team squads across formats.

## What the gala recognised

The award singled out three things: the medal pipeline from U13 through senior cricket, the strength of the women's programme, and the volunteer backbone — coaches, scorers, groundstaff and parents — who make a club of this size run six days a week from April to September.

## Looking ahead to 2026

Pre-season nets begin in February. The senior squad returns largely unchanged, with two Danish-qualified additions from the junior ranks. The women's team is targeting gold. And the U18s, who lost the bronze-medal match by seven runs in 2025, have already announced their intent for a different colour next September.
