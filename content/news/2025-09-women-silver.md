A breakthrough campaign ended in silver at the Danish women's championship in September, with Glostrup falling just short in the final against a strong Copenhagen side. The result is the best finish in the women's programme's history and sets up an obvious title push for 2026.

Captain Priya Anand called it "the season we proved we belong." Three of the squad's players — Anand herself, opener Mette Holm, and allrounder Sara Lind — were named to the Danish squad for the Nordic Cup later in the autumn.
