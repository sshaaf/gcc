title: "Youth medals keep coming"
tag: "Youth"
date: "2025-08-21"
excerpt: "Bronze for both U15 and U18 at DM 2025 — the academy pipeline is delivering."
---

Both Glostrup youth sides came home from the Danish youth championships with bronze medals in August, continuing a run of national-medal finishes that now stretches back six seasons.

The U15s, coached by Kamran Ahmed, beat three of the four seeded sides before falling in the semi-final. The U18s, led by captain Magnus Berg, lost their bronze-medal match by seven runs in a tight finish — but the squad returns almost intact for 2026 and has already set its sights on gold.
